# Requirements: Nocturne Night v2, "The Last Guest"

## Introduction

v2 rebuilds the other side of Nocturne Night from scratch as one continuous first-person 3D experience in a house that feels real. It keeps the v1 story and rules: the Devil hosts the party, the party ends at 3:00 AM, you have to escape, and the invitation comes first. It is built on what horror research and practice agree on:

- **Anticipation beats threats.** The monster is mostly glimpsed: at the end of a hall, gone when the light comes back (Silent Hill's off-screen Pyramid Head).
- **The ordinary place, made wrong.** One L-shaped hallway you walk again and again. Each lap something is different (P.T.).
- **Jump scares are rare and earned.** Constant scares dull each other, so most laps are quiet. The scares land at relief moments.
- **Asymmetry and the uncanny.** Nothing is symmetric. Figures are almost human.
- **Then the world turns over.** Partway through, the hallway flips. You are walking on the ceiling of the Underside.

v1 stays playable at the project root. v2 lives in `v2/`, reuses the shared assets and audio engine, and replaces v1 only once it plays end to end.

## Requirements

### R1 Entry and safety
1. WHEN v2 loads, THE page SHALL show a short content warning with **Enter** and **Gentle**, and SHALL start no audio before a click.
2. Gentle mode and `prefers-reduced-motion` SHALL remove jump scares, gore and flashes, quieten the audio, and add time.
3. THE page SHALL never flash more than 3 times per second. `Esc` SHALL mute, and a visible Leave link SHALL exist at all times.

### R2 Movement
1. THE visitor SHALL walk by scrolling up (the world is upside down), by holding `W` or `↑`, or by touch-dragging. Walking speed SHALL be capped, so scrolling hard never skips content.
2. THE pointer SHALL turn the head within a limited cone (about 60° left and right, 30° up and down). The camera path SHALL be fixed: no free roaming, no getting lost.
3. Interactive objects SHALL highlight on hover and respond to click or tap. Every lock SHALL also work by keyboard.

### R3 The loop (P.T.-style)
1. THE hallway SHALL be a short L: an entrance, a long corridor with doors, framed photographs, a side table with a radio, a turn, and a final door. Walking through the final door SHALL return the visitor to the entrance as the next lap.
2. EACH lap SHALL change the house: lights, sounds, photographs, doors left ajar, the radio's broadcast, a figure glimpsed. At least 6 laps SHALL exist, escalating from "almost normal" to "unbearable".
3. A lap SHALL NOT end until its lock (R5) is solved.

### R4 The Underside (the world turns over)
1. ON lap 4, THE house SHALL turn upside down in a single continuous camera roll. From then on, the furniture hangs from the floor above, the lamp stands upward, and the photographs are inverted.
2. THE inversion SHALL be visible and physical, not a screen flip: objects fall upward, the chandelier is now standing, and blood drips upward.

### R5 Locks inside the house (one per lap)
1. Lap 1: answer the ringing phone (it says your name).
2. Lap 2: turn off the radio (it will not stay off).
3. Lap 3: find the host's photograph among the frames.
4. Lap 4: wind the music box in the nursery behind a door (the doll).
5. Lap 5: choose the right door (hesitation is punished).
6. Lap 6: cancel your RSVP to the Devil (only your signed-up name works).
7. Wrong choices SHALL bring the hammer or a 3D gore set-piece. Correct choices SHALL sometimes bring an ambush and the "well done" hand.

### R6 The threat
1. THE thing SHALL be a real rigged 3D model. It is glimpsed at distance in lap 2, closer each time the light fails in lap 3, and behind you in lap 5 (seen only when the visitor turns).
2. THE climax (lap 6) SHALL be the full 3D confrontation, ending in the face.

### R7 Time and the end
1. A countdown SHALL show the time left until 3:00 AM. At zero, THE long ending SHALL play, then return to the invitation.
2. Escaping SHALL turn the world right side up and return to the invitation, with one wrong detail.

### R8 Assets and rules
1. Every asset SHALL be original, public domain or CC0, CC BY with credit, or generated by the team, and SHALL be listed in `ASSETS.md`. Nothing from `CreepyImages/` that belongs to someone else.
2. No new paid AI generation for this build. Use existing generated images, voices and the 2 remaining credits only for one or two key images.
3. Static files only, with no build step. three.js and postprocessing are vendored. 50 fps or better on a laptop, with adaptive quality.
4. Text SHALL always be readable above every effect.
